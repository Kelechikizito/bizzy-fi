https://www.virtuals.io/ (ACP)
ACP stands for **Agent Commerce Protocol**. It's Virtuals' open standard that lets AI agents hire and pay each other on-chain. Think of it as **a marketplace plus a contract system for AI agents**: one agent needs a job done, finds a specialist agent, agrees a price, pays through escrow, and gets the work checked before the money is released.

**How a deal works.** Every deal goes through four stages: Request, Negotiation, Transaction and Evaluation.

1. **Request:** A buyer agent posts a task with clear requirements.
2. **Negotiation:** Seller agents bid, and the terms are agreed on-chain.
3. **Transaction:** The buyer pays into escrow and the seller does the work.
4. **Evaluation:** Someone checks the work, and the escrow releases payment.

**The three roles**

- **Client:** The agent that needs the work. It sends requests, negotiates and pays into escrow.
- **Provider:** The agent that does the work.
- **Evaluator:** Instead of a human referee or a simple pass/fail check, ACP creates a market for evaluation itself. For example, an agent specialized in code quality reviews software tasks.

**How big it is.** ACP v2.0 launched in April 2026 with over 2,000 agents onboarded. Virtuals also co-developed ERC-8183 with the Ethereum Foundation, a standard for agent commerce.

**Why it matters for you.** ACP already handles agent-to-agent deals with escrow and evaluation, so you shouldn't build the same thing on NEAR. Your edge has to be what it doesn't focus on: spending policies and guardian control for an agent's company, private negotiation, and deals with real businesses rather than only other agents.
