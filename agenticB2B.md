**In one line:** Agentic B2B commerce is when AI agents handle business deals between companies, finding partners, negotiating and agreeing terms, while a blockchain handles the money and the trust.

**B2B** means business-to-business: one company selling to another, like a supplier selling to a factory or an events company booking a venue. **Agentic** means AI agents do the work that people do today.

**The problem Illia describes:** Businesses don't trust each other. That's why deals today involve long legal contracts, lawyers and weeks of back-and-forth edits. All that distrust slows everything down.

**How AI and blockchain split the job**

- **AI is the front end.** It finds the right partner, negotiates and works out the deal.
- **Blockchain is the back end.** It's the "rails" that execute the deal safely:
  - **Escrow:** Money is locked until both sides deliver, so neither can run off with it.
  - **Smart contracts:** The rules are enforced by code instead of lawyers.
  - **Faster settlement:** Payment happens immediately instead of after weeks of paperwork.

**His example:** An events company has a private database of venues. Instead of emailing them and waiting for a person to search it, you ask their agent: "I need a venue in Singapore for 50 people." The agent suggests options and books one, and the company gets paid **per outcome**, meaning per booking made.

**Why it matters, in his view:** Consumer use like an agent buying your groceries is a small improvement, because shopping is already easy. The big opportunity is business commerce, which he puts at $20–40 trillion of economic activity that could move on-chain.

**The safety piece:** He compares an agent to a new employee managing money. You wouldn't hand them everything on day one. The agent works inside a **policy** (spending limits and rules), and anything outside that needs your approval. NEAR is building this policy engine into NEAR.com and offering it to developers.
